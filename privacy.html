Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers personal development tools and utilities operated by Chris Somm. These are private tools built for individual use. They are not offered to the public and have no external users.

What these tools access

Certain tools authenticate to Google Drive in order to read and write files belonging solely to the operator of the tool. Access is limited to the operator's own Google account. No other person's account, data, or files are accessed.

What is collected

No personal information is collected from any third party. These tools have no users other than the operator, no analytics, no tracking, and no account registration.

What is stored

Authentication tokens issued by Google are stored locally on the operator's own computer for the sole purpose of maintaining access to the operator's own Google Drive. Tokens are not transmitted anywhere else.

What is shared

Nothing. No data is sold, shared, transferred, or disclosed to any third party.

Data retention and removal

Access can be revoked at any time by the operator through their Google Account security settings. Doing so immediately ends the tool's ability to access any Google data.
